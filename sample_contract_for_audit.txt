The Employer and Employee may collectively be referred to as the "Parties".

WHEREAS the Company wishes to employ the Employee in the capacity of Senior 
Software Architect, and the Employee desires to accept such employment under the 
terms and conditions set forth herein:

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties mutually agree as follows:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLAUSE 1: POSITION, COMMENCEMENT AND PROBATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Position: The Employee is hired as Senior Software Architect.
1.2 Commencement Date: Employment shall commence on November 1, 2026.
1.3 Probationary Period: The Employee shall serve an initial probationary period 
    of six (6) months, extendable at the sole discretion of the Company.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLAUSE 2: REMUNERATION AND BENEFITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Basic Salary: The Employee shall receive a consolidated monthly gross salary 
    of BDT 1,80,000 (One Lac Eighty Thousand Taka), subject to standard tax 
    deductions at source as per Income Tax Act, 2023.
2.2 Festival Bonus: Eligible for two festival bonuses per calendar year after 
    successful completion of confirmation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLAUSE 3: WORKING HOURS AND OVERTIME [LEGAL ISSUE #1]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Mandatory Hours: The Employee agrees to work a mandatory minimum of 65 
    (sixty-five) hours per week, including alternate Fridays and public holidays 
    whenever project deadlines require, without entitlement to any overtime pay 
    or compensatory leave.
3.2 Waiver of Overtime: The Employee expressly waives any right to overtime 
    allowances under any labor statute, agreeing that the executive nature of 
    the salary covers all extended work hours.
    [*CONFLICT: Violates Bangladesh Labour Act 2006, Sections 100, 102 & 108 
      (Max 48 hours/week, mandatory 2x wage rate for overtime).*]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLAUSE 4: POST-TERMINATION NON-COMPETE RESTRICTION [LEGAL ISSUE #2]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 5-Year Blanket Ban: The Employee covenants and agrees that for a period of 
    five (5) consecutive years following the cessation of employment for any 
    reason whatsoever, the Employee shall NOT directly or indirectly:
    (a) Establish, advise, consult, invest in, or work for any software, tech, 
        fintech, or IT consulting company operating anywhere within the territory 
        of Bangladesh;
    (b) Solicit or contact any client or business partner of the Company.
4.2 Injunctive Relief: The Employee agrees that any breach of this non-compete 
    restriction shall result in an immediate ex-parte injunction and liquidated 
    damages.
    [*CONFLICT: Violates Section 27 of The Contract Act, 1872 (Restraint of 
      trade is void under Bangladesh sovereign law).*]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLAUSE 5: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND ASSIGNMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Assignment: All code, documentation, algorithms, inventions, and patentable 
    works conceived or developed during employment shall remain the sole property 
    of the Employer.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLAUSE 6: CONFIDENTIALITY AND TRADE SECRETS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Non-Disclosure: The Employee shall maintain strict secrecy regarding all 
    proprietary technical architectures, algorithms, financial records, and 
    client identities during and after employment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLAUSE 7: TERMINATION AND NOTICE PERIOD [LEGAL ISSUE #3]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 Unilateral Company Termination: The Company reserves the absolute right to 
    terminate the Employee's service at any time with 24 (twenty-four) hours' 
    verbal or electronic (WhatsApp/Email) notice without showing any cause, and 
    without any obligation to pay severance, service compensation, or wages in 
    lieu of notice.
7.2 Employee Resignation: In stark contrast, if the Employee wishes to resign, 
    the Employee MUST provide a minimum of one hundred and eighty (180) days' 
    prior written notice. Failure to serve the full 180-day notice shall result 
    in immediate forfeiture of all unpaid salaries, provident fund benefits, 
    and earned leave encashment.
    [*CONFLICT: Violates Bangladesh Labour Act 2006, Section 26 & Section 27 
      (Mandatory 120 days' notice/wages in lieu for permanent workers; unlawful 
      arbitrary wage forfeiture).*]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLAUSE 8: SUMMARY DISMISSAL WITHOUT INVESTIGATION [LEGAL ISSUE #4]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.1 Immediate Dismissal: The Employer may summarily dismiss the Employee for any 
    alleged misconduct, insubordination, or performance shortfall without 
    instituting any domestic inquiry, formal charge-sheet, or opportunity to 
    show cause.
    [*CONFLICT: Violates Bangladesh Labour Act 2006, Section 23 & 24 
      (Mandatory disciplinary procedure, charge-sheet, and inquiry committee).*]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLAUSE 9: LIQUIDATED DAMAGES AND PENALTY BOND [LEGAL ISSUE #5]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9.1 Fixed Penalty: In the event the Employee departs the Company prior to 
    completing thirty-six (36) continuous months of service, or breaches any 
    covenant in this Agreement, the Employee shall pay to the Employer a fixed 
    punitive penalty of BDT 25,00,000 (Twenty-Five Lac Taka) as liquidated 
    damages, irrespective of whether the Employer suffered any actual financial 
    loss.
    [*CONFLICT: Violates Section 74 of The Contract Act, 1872 (Stipulation 
      by way of penalty is unenforceable; courts only grant reasonable actual 
      compensation).*]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLAUSE 10: DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND EXCLUSION OF LOCAL COURTS [LEGAL ISSUE #6]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.1 Exclusion of Bangladesh Jurisdiction: Any dispute, controversy, or claim 
     arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be exclusively submitted 
     to the courts of Singapore. The Parties explicitly agree to exclude the 
     jurisdiction of all labor tribunals and civil courts in Bangladesh.
     [*CONFLICT: Violates Section 28 of The Contract Act, 1872 (Agreements 
       restraining legal proceedings in local courts are void).*]
